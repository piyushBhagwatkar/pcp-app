About: why three outcomes?

PCP is undecidable but semi-decidable. If a solution exists, BFS will eventually find it (and it will be the shortest). If none exists, the search may never stop on its own, so a depth limit is needed. A result of No solution is only reported when it is proven (every branch pruned, or a length argument). Otherwise the honest answer is Inconclusive.
